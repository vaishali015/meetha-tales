Our Sweet Story

Meetha Tales began as a small family-run sweet shop with a simple mission: to bring the authentic taste of traditional Indian sweets to every home. For over three decades, we have been crafting sweets using time-honored recipes passed down through generations.

Our master sweet makers start before dawn each day, preparing fresh batches of pedha, laddu, jalebi, and many more delights. We use only the finest ingredients - pure cow ghee, fresh milk from local farms, premium cashews and almonds, and authentic cardamom and saffron.

From everyday treats to festival specials and wedding bulk orders, Meetha Tales has become a trusted name for sweet lovers across Bengaluru and beyond.
